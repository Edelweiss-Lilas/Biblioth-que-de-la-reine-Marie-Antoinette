# Bibliothèque de la reine Marie-Antoinette
I chose to work on a corpus of three historical catalogs of Queen Marie-Antoinette. These three documents describes diffrent aspects of her book collections and allow us to see her evolution as a reader and the kinds of works she enjoyed. This documents are _Livres du boudoir de la reine Marie-Antoinette_ by Louis Lacour, _Bibliothèque de la reine Marie-Antoinette au Petit Trianon_ by Paul Lacroix, and _Bibliothèque de la reine Marie-Antoinette au Château des Tuileries_by Ernest Quentin-Bauchart. I selected this documents to show on new image of this famous queen. Theses catalogues record the literary and material contents of the Queen's private and royal libraries.
I will use XML to help to structure this project. XML can represent the hierarchical organization already present in the catalogs (title, author,format, bookbinding, origin). It will also male it easier to navigate between the different collections originating from distinct places (Boudoir, Trianon, Tuileries). XML standardizes the bibliograhpic data, making the Queen's dispersed collections easir to analyse and compare digitally. I will select several entries from various sections of each catalogue. Each entry will describe a specific book or set of volumes, including relevant bibliographic information.
To encode them, I will create a Document Type Definiton (DTD) that defines core bibliographic elements. The XML template will demonstrate how these elements interact within a <listBibl> structure. Together, these files will illustrate how the inventories of Queen Marie-Antoinette's libraries can be structured and encoded.
